Cancellation policy

Plain rules, no fine print. PhotoPholio is a demo, so no real money moves, but the app follows exactly what is written here.

How a booking works

  1. You send a request for a date. Nothing is charged.
  2. The photographer accepts or declines.
  3. If accepted, you have {{ window }} hours to pay. If you do not, the request lapses and nothing is charged.
  4. After payment the booking is confirmed.

What you pay, and who gets it

You pay the photographer's price for the shoot (one day's price for each day) plus travel and stay if the event is outside their city. You see the full total before you send a request, and there is no extra fee for you. PhotoPholio keeps {{ commission }}% of the photographer's price as commission, and the photographer receives the rest and all of the travel money. The photographer's share is held until your shoot is completed.

Changing your date

Need a different day? Once the photographer has accepted, you can ask to move your booking, as long as the shoot is {{ resched_days }} or more days away. They need to agree and be free on the new day. Your price and payment stay as they are, and a booking can be moved {{ resched_max }} time{{ resched_max|pluralize }}. If they say no, you keep your original date. If you cancel later, the refund is worked out from the date your booking has then.

If the photographer cancels

You get a 100% refund, whatever the date. Every cancellation by a photographer is recorded and reviewed by the admin.

If the photographer does not show up

Contact us. After we look into it you get a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Cancelling and refunding are two steps. When you cancel, the refund shows as pending. It changes to refunded once the admin has processed it.
