Cancellation policy

Plain rules, no fine print. PhotoPholio is a demo, so no real money moves, but the app follows exactly what is written here.

How a booking works

  1. You send a request for a date. Nothing is charged.
  2. The photographer accepts or declines.
  3. If accepted, you have {{ window }} hours to pay. If you do not, the request lapses and nothing is charged.
  4. After payment the booking is confirmed.

What you pay, and who gets it

You pay the photographer's price for the shoot (one day's price for each day) plus travel and stay if the event is outside their city. You see the full total before you send a request, and there is no extra fee for you. PhotoPholio keeps {{ commission }}% of the photographer's price as commission, and the photographer receives the rest and all of the travel money. The photographer's share is held until your shoot is completed.

Printed albums

An album is optional and is paid for with the booking. It is priced separately from the photographer and never changes the shoot price or its refund.

  • Where the album price goes. It covers the printing and delivery cost. What is left is shared: the photographer who designs your album gets {{ album_share }}% as a design fee, and PhotoPholio keeps the other {{ album_rest }}% as its album commission.
  • Cancelling. The album is only made after the shoot. If the booking is cancelled, the album is cancelled too and you get the whole album price back, so nobody keeps the commission.
  • Once you approve the design, it goes to print and cannot be cancelled. Up to {{ album_rounds }} rounds of changes are free before that.
  • Delivery. Your album reaches you within {{ album_days }} days of your approval. If it is late, write to us in the Help center and we will tell you why.
  • The photographer's design fee is released when you approve the design, and it is paid with their next payout.

Your photos stay yours

The photos from your shoot are private to you and your photographer. Nobody else can open your gallery.

After your final delivery, whether it is the digital photos, the album or both, the photographer must not keep, share, post, sell or reuse them anywhere (portfolio, social media, ads, contests, stock sites) without your clear written permission. They must also delete the copies from their phone, computer and cloud storage.

This is a rule for every photographer on PhotoPholio. If you find your photos used without your permission, tell us in the Help center. We look into every report. A photographer who misuses your photos is removed from PhotoPholio, and we can give you the details you need to take legal action under the law that applies.

Changing your date

Need a different day? Once the photographer has accepted, you can ask to move your booking, as long as the shoot is {{ resched_days }} or more days away. They need to agree and be free on the new day. Your price and payment stay as they are, and a booking can be moved {{ resched_max }} time{{ resched_max|pluralize }}. If they say no, you keep your original date. If you cancel later, the refund is worked out from the date your booking has then.

If the photographer cancels

You get a 100% refund, whatever the date. Every cancellation by a photographer is recorded and reviewed by the admin.

If the photographer does not show up

Contact us. After we look into it you get a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Cancelling and refunding are two steps. When you cancel, the refund shows as pending. It changes to refunded once the admin has processed it.
